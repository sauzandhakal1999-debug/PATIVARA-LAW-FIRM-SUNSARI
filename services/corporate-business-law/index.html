Understanding the Legal Problem & Client Need

Doing business in Nepal requires navigating an evolving regulatory framework across multiple ministries and statutory bodies, including the Office of Company Registrar (OCR), Department of Industry (DOI), Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB), and the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). Non-compliance with mandatory annual filings, shareholder meeting protocols, director fiduciary duties, or foreign investment repatriation rules can trigger severe financial penalties, corporate deadlock, or director disqualification. Strategic corporate structuring from the outset shields promoters and creates a scalable foundation for growth.

Important Legal Rights, Duties & Procedural Limitations

  • • Directors Fiduciary Standard: Under Section 99 of the Companies Act 2063, directors owe strict fiduciary duties to the company and are personally liable for actions taken in conflict of interest or in breach of statutory limits.
  • • Mandatory Annual Filings: Companies must submit audited balance sheets, auditor reports, and annual return forms to the OCR within six months of fiscal year close; late filings incur compounding statutory fines.
  • • Foreign Investment Thresholds: Foreign direct investment is subject to statutory minimum investment thresholds and negative list restrictions established by the Government of Nepal.
  • • Separate Legal Entity Principle: The corporate veil protects shareholders from personal liability for company obligations, provided corporate governance formalities are duly observed.

Common Questions Regarding Corporate & Business Law Advisory

How long does it take to register a Private Limited Company in Nepal?

With all promoter identification and charter documents prepared, company registration at the Office of Company Registrar (OCR) typically completes within 5 to 10 working days, followed by approximately 3 to 5 days for PAN/VAT and ward registration.

Can a foreign individual or foreign entity own 100% of a company in Nepal?

Yes, in most commercial sectors, 100% foreign equity ownership is permitted under FITTA 2075, subject to minimum statutory capital thresholds (currently NPR 20 million per foreign investor) and compliance with the negative list (which excludes sectors like primary agriculture, retail trade, and arms).

What are the essential annual filings required for companies at the OCR in Nepal?

Every registered company must file annual returns including: notice of Annual General Meeting (Form 21/16); list of shareholders and directors (Form 9); audited financial statements; and auditor appointment notices within six months of fiscal year completion.

How are shareholder disputes resolved under the Companies Act 2063?

Shareholder disputes can be resolved through provisions defined in a customized Shareholder Agreement, internal board dispute mechanisms, or by filing an oppression petition before the High Court Commercial Bench under Sections 139 and 140 of the Companies Act 2063.
