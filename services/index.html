Legal Practice Areas in Nepal

Structured legal advisory, contract drafting, and courtroom advocacy across all major civil, criminal, commercial, and property disciplines under Nepalese law.

Core Practice Area

Civil Litigation & Dispute Resolution

We provide robust, strategic representation in civil disputes across District Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court of Nepal. Our civil litigation practice helps individuals, property owners, and businesses assert legal rights, recover monetary claims, obtain urgent court injunctions, and defend against civil claims under the National Civil Code and Civil Procedure Code 2074.

Key Focus: Commercial contract breaches, supplier non-performance, and partnership fallout

Core Practice Area

Criminal Law & Defence Representation

We offer vigilant, ethical criminal defence representation for individuals, corporate officers, and accused persons facing investigation or formal prosecution by the Nepal Police and Office of the Attorney General. From police questioning and judicial remand (Thap Myad) to bail hearings (Dharauti/Jamanat) and District Court trials, we safeguard your constitutional rights under the National Criminal Procedure Code 2074.

Key Focus: Police questioning, notices to appear, or urgent arrest and detention by Nepal Police

Core Practice Area

Family Law & Divorce Representation

Family disputes require high legal precision handled with privacy and compassion. Our family law practice assists spouses and families navigating divorce (Sambandh Bichhed), child custody and guardianship, spousal maintenance (Kharacha), and matrimonial property partition (Ansha) under the Muluki Civil Code 2074. We represent clients in District Courts as well as confidential structured mediation.

Key Focus: Mutual consent divorce where both spouses agree on settlement terms and wish to conclude swiftly

Core Practice Area

Property & Land Law Representation

Land and real estate represent the most valuable asset class in Nepal, yet they are fraught with administrative complexity and inheritance disputes. We provide rigorous legal due diligence for property purchases, title deed verification (Lalpurja examination), representation at the Land Revenue Office (Malpot Karyalaya), ancestral partition litigation (Anshabanda Mudda), and boundary dispute resolution under the Land Act 2021 and Muluki Civil Code 2074.

Key Focus: Pre-purchase title due diligence, ownership chain verification, and Malpot record verification

Core Practice Area

Corporate & Business Law Advisory

We serve as trusted outside corporate counsel to startups, private enterprises, joint ventures, and international investors operating in Nepal. Our corporate legal practice encompasses company incorporation at the Office of Company Registrar (OCR), Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) approvals, corporate restructuring, regulatory compliance, commercial contracts, and shareholder dispute resolution under the Companies Act 2063.

Key Focus: Incorporating Private Limited Companies, Public Companies, or Branch/Liaison Offices in Nepal

Core Practice Area

Contract Drafting & Agreement Review

A well-drafted contract is your primary shield against future disputes. We draft, review, and negotiate commercial agreements, vendor contracts, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), joint venture covenants, and leases under the Muluki Civil Code 2074. Our contracts are meticulously tailored to Nepalese statutory requirements to ensure full enforceability in court.

Key Focus: Drafting commercial vendor agreements, supply chain contracts, and service level agreements (SLAs)

Core Practice Area

Inheritance & Succession Law

Succession and ancestral property partition represent the most frequent civil disputes in Nepalese society. We assist heirs, surviving spouses, daughters, and families in determining lawful inheritance rights (Apunstrali / Hakwala), formalizing ancestral partition (Anshabanda), drafting wills (Ichhapatra), and executing property mutations under the Muluki Civil Code 2074.

Key Focus: Partition of ancestral estate (Anshabanda) among lawful co-heirs (Anshiyars)
Review Full Practice Scope & Legal Process →
Core Practice Area

Labour & Employment Law Representation

Balancing fair employee relations with regulatory compliance is vital for any commercial enterprise. We provide comprehensive employment legal counsel to corporations, organizations, and senior executives under the Labour Act 2074 and Social Security Act 2074. Our practice spans employment agreement drafting, workplace policy audits, disciplinary procedures, termination counseling, and representation before the Labour Court and Labour Offices.

Key Focus: Drafting employment contracts, executive service agreements, and consultancy covenants
Review Full Practice Scope & Legal Process →
Core Practice Area

Banking & Financial Dispute Representation

Financial disputes demand strategic evaluation of both civil recovery remedies and criminal penal provisions. We represent businesses, commercial creditors, borrowers, and corporate entities in banking disputes, cheque dishonor (cheque bounce) cases, loan recovery proceedings before the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT), and collateral auction disputes under the Banking Offence and Punishment Act 2064 and Negotiable Instruments Act 2034.

Key Focus: Recovering funds from dishonored commercial cheques via criminal or civil legal remedies

Core Practice Area

Mediation & Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

Court litigation can be time-consuming, adversarial, and public. We offer skilled, confidential Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) representation in commercial arbitration and professional mediation under the Mediation Act 2068 and Arbitration Act 2055. We help corporate entities, partners, and families resolve high-stakes disputes amicably, efficiently, and with binding legal enforceability.

Key Focus: Commercial partnership and shareholder disputes requiring confidential resolution without public exposure
